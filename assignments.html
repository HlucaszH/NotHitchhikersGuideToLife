Assignment 2: Placement Test (A1–C2)

Level A1

Task 1: Basics

Complete the sentences: "My name ___ Anna. I ___ 20 years old. My favourite colour ___ blue."

Level A2

Task 2: Your Day

Score _____ / 5 (teacher use)

Level B1

Task 3: Experience vs. Event

Write two sentences: one using present perfect ("I have ever…") and one using past simple, about the same topic.

Score _____ / 5 (teacher use)

Level B2

Task 4: Short Opinion

In 4–5 sentences, give your opinion on something you've watched, read, or listened to recently. Use at least one linking word (however, because, although…).

Score _____ / 5 (teacher use)

Level C1

Task 5: Rephrase It

Rewrite this sentence in a more formal way: "This thing you sent me doesn't really work, can you fix it?"

Score _____ / 5 (teacher use)

Level C2

Task 6: Make Your Case

In about 100 words, argue for or against this statement: "Learning a language from a textbook is a waste of time." Use natural, idiomatic English.

Score _____ / 5 (teacher use)
