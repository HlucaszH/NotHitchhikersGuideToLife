About the lessons

What to expect if you sign up

Every course is built around the student, not a fixed textbook. We start with a short assessment of your current level, then plan lessons around what you actually need: everyday conversation, business English, exam preparation, or simply confidence.

Lessons run online or in person, once or twice a week, 45–60 minutes each. Every lesson comes with short homework so what you practice in class doesn't fade by the next session — you'll find the current material on the Lessons and Assignments pages.
